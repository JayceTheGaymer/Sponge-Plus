# Sponge Plus

A Minecraft (NeoForge and Fabric) mod that improves on base sponge mechanics. Supports 1.21.1 and 26.2, open a GitHub issue if you would like me to look into another version to add.

## Summary

Sponge Plus spurred from a Reddit post where someone was asking for Mojang to add sponge drying mechanics to the Magma Block. Many mods that improved on sponges appear to be abandoned projects, so I decided to make Sponge Plus!

## Features

- Placing Wet Sponges around Magma Blocks or Campfires will cause them to convert to dry sponges after a few seconds. No more trips to the Nether to dry them or wasting fuel in a furnace.
- Leaving a Wet Sponge out in the sun will cause them to dry out after a few minutes. Inversely, leaving a dry Sponge out in the rain will cause it to become wet after a few minutes.
- Sponges can now be found in loot chests in ship wrecks and underwater ruins at a low rate. This allows them to be obtained outside of Ocean Monuments.
